How the valley grows

You have twelve hands. Choose a district, hold the cards you like, and draw up to three. The completed hand becomes a building. Suits feed their matching district; your chosen district breaks ties.

High card → Homestead+1 renown · a modest start
Pair → Local business+3 renown · bakery, chandlery, shop or kiln
Two pair → Town block+6 renown · rowhouses and active streets
Three of a kind → Guildhall+10 renown · a specialist institution
Straight → Station+15 renown · roads connect the valley
Flush → District hall+22 renown · one district flourishes
Full house → Grand hotel+30 renown · a destination appears
Four of a kind → Playhouse+42 renown · rare civic architecture
Straight flush → Wonder+65 renown · the valley's defining landmark
